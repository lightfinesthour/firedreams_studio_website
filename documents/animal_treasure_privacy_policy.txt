PRIVACY POLICY FOR ANIMAL TREASURE
==================================

App:         Animal Treasure (studio.firedreams.animalfinder)
Developer:   Firedreams Studio
Contact:     dream@firedreams.studio
Effective:   9 November 2026
Last update: 9 November 2026


SUMMARY
-------
Animal Treasure is a picture and sound game for young children. It
collects no data. None. The app has no internet access, no accounts,
no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no in-app purchases and no
third-party services.


DETAILS
-------

1. Data collection
   Animal Treasure does not ask for, collect, store or transmit any
   personal data or any other data. The app requests no permissions at
   all (no internet, camera, microphone, location or contacts), so it
   is technically unable to send anything off your device.

2. Game content
   All photos, sounds, voices and pictures are bundled inside the app
   itself. Nothing is downloaded, and no request ever leaves your
   device while your child plays.

3. Settings and sticker book
   Two things are saved, on your device only:
   - the parent settings (language, volume, difficulty, which animal
     groups are shown and the other options in the parent area);
   - the sticker book (which stickers have been won, where they are
     stuck, and a counter of playing time used to hand out new
     stickers).
   They are never sent anywhere, and they are deleted when you
   uninstall the app.

4. Third parties
   The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party
   code that collects data. Distribution of the app is handled entirely
   by Google Play; see Google's privacy policy for how Google processes
   downloads.

5. Children
   The app is made for young children. Since no data is collected from
   anyone, no data is collected from children. The parent settings are
   behind a parent gate (press and hold the gear button).

6. Changes
   If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at
   this same address with an updated date.

7. Contact
   Questions? Email dream@firedreams.studio.
