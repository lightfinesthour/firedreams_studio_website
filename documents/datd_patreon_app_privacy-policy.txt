2. WHAT DATA WE COLLECT

We collect only the data needed to run our business:

- Contact details (name, email address) when you email us or contact us
  through a platform.
- Order and payment information when you buy our game or support us
  through a platform such as Steam, itch.io, or Patreon. Payments are
  processed by those platforms. We receive the details needed to deliver
  your purchase and meet our tax obligations.
- Newsletter data (email address) if you subscribe to updates, and only
  with your consent.
- Website usage data if our website uses analytics. If it does, this
  policy will name the analytics tool and what it collects.

3. WHAT WE USE YOUR DATA FOR

- Delivering purchases and rewards you are entitled to.
- Answering your messages.
- Sending newsletters or updates you signed up for. Every message
  includes an unsubscribe link.
- Meeting legal obligations, such as Dutch tax administration
  (Belastingdienst), which requires us to keep sales records for 7 years.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers.

4. PATRON PAGE AND PATRON EMAILS (DEMON AT THE DOOR)

Patreon supporters of "Demon at the Door" get a patron page at
patrons.demonatthedoor.com, where you log in with your Patreon account.
To run it we receive from Patreon, and store:

- Your Patreon member and user ID, name, email address, membership
  status and tier.
- Your Discord user ID, if you connected Discord in your Patreon
  settings.

When you use the service we also store:

- Which game builds you downloaded, for which platform, and when.
- Your votes in polls.
- The name you choose for the game's special thanks (higher tiers only).
  It stays in the credits after your membership ends.
- Your choice to receive or skip new-build emails.
- Copies of the emails and Discord messages we sent you.
- A login cookie (valid up to 30 days) that keeps you signed in. The
  page sets no tracking or advertising cookies.

We use this to deliver your tier's downloads, votes, Special Gallery
codes and credits, and to email you when you join, change tier or leave,
and when a new build is out (you can switch build emails off on the
patron page).

Processors: the server provider that hosts the patron page, Brevo
(Sendinblue SAS, France) which sends the emails, and Discord if you
connected it. Nightly backups of the patron database are kept for 14
days on the server and are also copied to the owner's computer.

These records are kept after a membership ends, so a returning patron
keeps their settings and credit name. Email us to have them deleted.
Deletion covers the live data right away and backups as they roll over.

5. THIRD-PARTY PLATFORMS

If you buy from us or support us through a third-party platform (Steam,
itch.io, Patreon, or similar), that platform processes your data under
its own privacy policy. We only receive the information the platform
shares with sellers and creators.

6. LEGAL BASIS (GDPR)

We process personal data on these grounds:

- Performance of a contract: delivering what you bought, including
  patron rewards and membership emails.
- Legal obligation: tax and accounting records.
- Consent: newsletters and optional updates. You can withdraw consent
  at any time.

7. RETENTION

- Tax and sales records: 7 years, as required by Dutch law.
- Email correspondence: up to 2 years after our last contact.
- Newsletter subscriptions: until you unsubscribe.
- Patron page records: see section 4.

8. YOUR RIGHTS

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you.
- Correct inaccurate data.
- Request deletion of your data, where the law allows it.
- Object to or restrict processing.
- Data portability.
- Lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority
  (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

To exercise any of these rights, email us at dream@firedreams.studio. We respond
within 30 days.

9. SECURITY

We store data on secured systems with access limited to the owner of
Firedreams Studio. Accounts holding personal data use strong passwords
and, where available, two-factor authentication.

10. CHANGES

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. For significant
changes we will announce the update on our website or through the
platform where you follow us.
