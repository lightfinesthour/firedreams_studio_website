2. WHAT DATA WE COLLECT

We collect only the data needed to run our business:

- Contact details (name, email address) when you email us or contact us
  through a platform.
- Order and payment information when you buy our game or support us
  through a platform such as Steam, itch.io, or Patreon. Payments are
  processed by those platforms. We receive the details needed to deliver
  your purchase and meet our tax obligations.
- Newsletter data (email address) if you subscribe to updates, and only
  with your consent.
- Website usage data if our website uses analytics. If it does, this
  policy will name the analytics tool and what it collects.

3. WHAT WE USE YOUR DATA FOR

- Delivering purchases and rewards you are entitled to.
- Answering your messages.
- Sending newsletters or updates you signed up for. Every message
  includes an unsubscribe link.
- Meeting legal obligations, such as Dutch tax administration
  (Belastingdienst), which requires us to keep sales records for 7 years.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers.

4. PATRON PAGE AND PATRON EMAILS (DEMON AT THE DOOR)

Patreon members of "Demon at the Door", on a paid tier or the free
tier, get a patron page at patrons.demonatthedoor.com, where you log in
with your Patreon account. To run it we receive from Patreon, and store:

- Your Patreon member and user ID, name, email address, membership
  status and tier.
- Your Discord user ID, if you connected Discord in your Patreon
  settings.

This includes free members: we store this record when you join the free
tier on Patreon, whether or not you visit the patron page.

When you use the service we also store:

- Which game builds you downloaded, for which platform, and when.
- Your votes in polls.
- The name you choose for the game's special thanks (higher tiers only).
  It stays in the credits after your membership ends.
- Your choice to receive or skip new-build emails.
- Copies of the emails and Discord messages we sent you.
- A login cookie (valid up to 30 days) that keeps you signed in. The
  page sets no tracking or advertising cookies.

We use this to deliver your tier's downloads, votes, Special Gallery
codes and credits, and to email you when you join, change tier or leave,
and when a new build is out (you can switch build emails off on the
patron page). Free members get the patron page and can vote in polls
that are open to them; we send free members no emails or Discord
messages.

Processors: the server provider that hosts the patron page, Brevo
(Sendinblue SAS, France) which sends the emails, and Discord if you
connected it. Nightly backups of the patron database are kept for 14
days on the server and are also copied to the owner's computer.

These records are kept after a membership ends, so a returning patron
keeps their settings and credit name. Email us to have them deleted.
Deletion covers the live data right away and backups as they roll over.

5. PLAY STATISTICS IN THE GAME (DEMON AT THE DOOR)

From the release after 0.1.2, "Demon at the Door" sends anonymous play
statistics to our server while you play. The game explains this on a
screen after the age check, the first time you start it, and you can
switch it off at any time in Options > Privacy.

What the game sends, about once a minute while statistics are on:

- A random ID the game creates on your device. It is not linked to your
  name, account, email or Patreon membership.
- A random ID for each time you start the game.
- The game version, platform (Windows, Linux, Android), the Linux
  distribution name (to recognise the Steam Deck) and the game language.
- Story progress: which story events you complete, the in-game day and
  room, starting a new game, loading a save, and game overs.
- Your hardware: graphics card name and vendor, video memory (rounded to
  MB), processor name and number of threads, memory (rounded to whole
  GB), main screen resolution and operating system version. We use it to
  see what kind of computer most players have, so the game runs well on
  them.

What it does not send: your name, account, email or save files.

Your IP address reaches our server with every message, as with any
internet connection. We use it only to look up the country (with the
DB-IP "IP to Country Lite" database, a file kept on our own server, so
your address is not sent to anyone), to limit how many messages one
address can send, and to leave out our own test devices. The address is
then discarded: it is not stored with
the statistics, and our server keeps no access log.

We use the statistics to see how many people play, for how long, where
they stop, and how to improve the game.

If you switch statistics off, the game sends one last message that only
says statistics were turned off, with the game version and platform and
no ID. It then sends nothing, and the random ID is deleted from your
device. Statistics already received stay, but they are not linked to
you.

Where it is kept: on the same server as the patron page, in a database
separate from patron data, and in the nightly backups described in
section 4. Only the owner of Firedreams Studio can view it.

6. THIRD-PARTY PLATFORMS

If you buy from us or support us through a third-party platform (Steam,
itch.io, Patreon, or similar), that platform processes your data under
its own privacy policy. We only receive the information the platform
shares with sellers and creators.

7. LEGAL BASIS (GDPR)

We process personal data on these grounds:

- Performance of a contract: delivering what you bought, including
  patron rewards and membership emails.
- Legal obligation: tax and accounting records.
- Consent: newsletters and optional updates. You can withdraw consent
  at any time.
- Legitimate interest: anonymous play statistics in the game (section
  5), to understand how the game is played and improve it. You can
  object at any time by switching statistics off in the game.
- Legitimate interest: the record of free Patreon members (section 4),
  so they can use the patron page and vote in polls open to them. You
  can ask us to delete it at any time.

8. RETENTION

- Tax and sales records: 7 years, as required by Dutch law.
- Email correspondence: up to 2 years after our last contact.
- Newsletter subscriptions: until you unsubscribe.
- Patron page records: see section 4.
- Play statistics: individual events (story progress, game overs) for
  180 days. Totals per random ID (first and last seen, play time,
  country, last story event reached, hardware) and opt-out counts are
  kept.

9. YOUR RIGHTS

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you.
- Correct inaccurate data.
- Request deletion of your data, where the law allows it.
- Object to or restrict processing.
- Data portability.
- Lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority
  (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

To exercise any of these rights, email us at dream@firedreams.studio. We respond
within 30 days. Play statistics carry no name or contact detail, so we
can only find yours if you send us the random ID from your game's
privacy.cfg file (in the game's user data folder).

10. SECURITY

We store data on secured systems with access limited to the owner of
Firedreams Studio. Accounts holding personal data use strong passwords
and, where available, two-factor authentication.

11. CHANGES

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. For significant
changes we will announce the update on our website or through the
platform where you follow us.
