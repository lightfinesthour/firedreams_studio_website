PRIVACY POLICY FOR DREAMCAMERA
==============================

App:         DreamCamera (studio.firedreams.dreamcamera)
Developer:   Firedreams Studio
Contact:     dream@firedreams.studio
Effective:   18 November 2026
Last update: 18 November 2026


SUMMARY
-------
DreamCamera collects no data. Your photos never leave your device unless
you share them yourself. The app has no internet access, no accounts,
no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party services.


DETAILS
-------

1. Data collection
   DreamCamera does not collect, store on any server, or transmit any
   personal data or any other data. The app does not request the
   internet permission, so it is technically unable to send anything
   off your device.

2. Camera
   The app uses the camera to show the viewfinder and take photos.
   The images are processed on your device only.

3. Photos
   Photos you take or import are stored in the app's private storage on
   your device, so you can redevelop them later. When you choose "Save
   to Photos" or turn on automatic saving, a copy is written to your
   device's photo gallery (album "DreamCamera"). Photos you import are
   read from your gallery only when you pick them.

4. Settings
   Your preferences (such as favourite gear, kits and settings) are
   stored locally on your device only.

5. Deleting your data
   Delete photos inside the app (they stay in "Recently deleted" for 30
   days, or delete them forever there), or uninstall the app to remove
   everything it stored. Copies you saved to your gallery stay there
   until you delete them.

6. Third parties
   The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party
   code that collects data. Your purchase of the app is handled entirely
   by Google Play; see Google's privacy policy for how Google processes
   payments.

7. Children
   The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including
   children.

8. Changes
   If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at
   this same address with an updated date.

9. Contact
   Questions? Email dream@firedreams.studio.
