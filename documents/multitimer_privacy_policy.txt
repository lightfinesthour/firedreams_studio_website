PRIVACY POLICY FOR MULTITIMER
==============================

App:         MultiTimer (studio.firedreams.multitimerpro)
Developer:   Firedreams Studio
Contact:     dream@firedreams.studio
Effective:   26 August 2026
Last update: 26 August 2026


SUMMARY
-------
MultiTimer collects no data. None. The app has no internet access,
no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party services.


DETAILS
-------

1. Data collection
   MultiTimer does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data
   or any other data. The app does not request the internet
   permission, so it is technically unable to send anything off your
   device.

2. Your timers and settings
   The timers you create — their names, durations, phases, sounds and
   alert options — along with your app preferences, are stored locally
   on your device only. They are never sent anywhere, and they are
   deleted when you uninstall the app.

3. Permissions
   The permissions MultiTimer asks for exist only to make timers go
   off at the right moment, and none of them are used to gather
   information:

     Notifications        to show running timers and to tell you when
                          one has finished.
     Alarms and reminders to fire a timer at the exact second it is
                          due, including while the phone is dozing.
     Full-screen alerts   to show a finished timer on a locked screen.
     Run at startup       to put running timers back on the clock
                          after you restart your phone.
     Vibration            to buzz when a timer finishes or is about to.

   MultiTimer uses a foreground service so that timers keep running
   accurately while the app is in the background. That service only
   counts time and updates its own notification.

4. Third parties
   The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party
   code that collects data. Your purchase of the app is handled
   entirely by Google Play; see Google's privacy policy for how Google
   processes payments.

5. Children
   The app is safe for all ages. Since no data is collected from
   anyone, no data is collected from children.

6. Your rights
   Because no data ever reaches us, there is nothing for us to look
   up, correct, export or erase on your behalf. Your data is on your
   device and under your control: clearing the app's storage or
   uninstalling it removes it completely.

7. Changes
   If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at
   this same address with an updated date.

8. Contact
   Questions? Email dream@firedreams.studio.
