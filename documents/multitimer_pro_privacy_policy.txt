Privacy Policy — MultiTimer Pro

Last updated: 5 September 2026

MultiTimer Pro (studio.firedreams.multitimerpro) is a timer app for Android.

What we collect

Nothing.

MultiTimer Pro does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app
does not request the Android internet permission and has no app-operated
servers or network data collection.

What the app stores on your device

The timers you create — their names, durations, colours, icons, alert settings —
and their current running state are saved in the app's own private storage on
your device. The app does not send this information to the developer.

If you have Android's own backup feature switched on for your Google account,
Android may include your timer settings in your device backup. That backup is
made by Android under your own Google account, is governed by Google's privacy
policy, and is not accessible to us.

Permissions and why they are needed

- Notifications — to show running timers and to alert you when one finishes.
- Foreground service — to keep timers counting while the app is in the
  background.
- Alarms and reminders — so a timer goes off at the right moment even when
  the device is in battery-saving Doze mode.
- Run at startup — to restore timers that were running before a restart.
- Vibrate — for the finish and pre-finish alerts.
- Full-screen intent — so a finished timer can show on the lock screen.

None of these permissions are used to gather information about you.

Third parties

MultiTimer Pro contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no
advertising or tracking SDKs. It uses AndroidX and Kotlin libraries for local
app functionality.

Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. Since it
collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes its local app data. Android backups may remain
under your Google account; manage or delete these in your device backup settings. You can also clear it at
any time from Android Settings → Apps → MultiTimer Pro → Storage → Clear storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same
address with a new "last updated" date.
